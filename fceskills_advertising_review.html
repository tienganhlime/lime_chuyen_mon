Invalid Question

Team loses the turn

💡 How to extend your answer: Direct answer → Reason (because…) → Example (For example…) → Feeling (I find it… / I think…)
  • Do you spend a lot of time online? What do you usually do?
  • What kind of ads do you usually see when you use your phone?
  • Have you ever bought something because of an ad? What was it?
  • Do you think ads are useful or annoying? Why?
  • Do you prefer watching TV channels with ads, or would you rather pay to avoid them?
  • What type of ads do you find most interesting? Why?
  • How do you usually react when an ad pops up on your screen?
  • Have you ever clicked on an online ad on purpose? What happened?
  • ✅ Ask about personal experience, habits, or opinions
  • ✅ Good starters: "Do you often…?" / "Have you ever…?" / "What kind of…?"
  • ❌ Don't ask about government policy or laws → that's Part 4
  • ❌ Don't ask to compare and choose between options → that's Part 3
  • ❌ Invalid example: "Should governments ban ads for children?"
  • ❌ Invalid example: "Which type of advertising is most effective?"
