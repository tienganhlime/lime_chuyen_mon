Part VI – Reading  (15 pts)
Last Saturday, my family decided to spend the day outdoors. We left home early in the morning and drove to a small town near a forest. The weather was perfect — the sun was shining and there was a light wind. We went hiking for two hours, then stopped next to a river to have a picnic. My mum made sandwiches and my dad brought some fruit. While we were eating, my little brother saw a deer in the trees! It looked at us for a moment, then ran away quickly. In the afternoon, we visited a small market in the town. I bought a beautiful wooden box for my collection. We arrived home late, but everyone was very happy.

A. Circle TRUE or FALSE. (5 pts)

1. The family went to the forest by train.
2. They had their picnic near a river.
3. The mother brought fruit for the picnic.
4. The little brother saw an animal in the forest.
5. They visited a market in the afternoon.

B. Answer in FULL sentences. (10 pts)

1. What was the weather like that day?

2. How long did they go hiking?

3. What did the narrator buy at the market?

4. Why do you think the deer ran away? Give a reason.

5. Do you think this was a good day for the family? Why or why not?
